## Verification cautions

- “Green bonds” refers to the IMF’s recorded issuance construct, not every form of green finance.
- The IMF geography is the issuer’s country of incorporation. It does not establish domestic use of proceeds.
- The IMF/LSEG redistribution terms for the raw green-bond observations remain to be cleared before a public reproducibility release. The article may cite the official IMF source; the public repository should not redistribute the raw file unless permission is confirmed.
- Institutional records without journal DOIs are cited through their official persistent URLs.
