# Project Instructions

## Project identity

- **Locked empirical title:** *Green Bonds without Fossil Exit? Physical Transition Credibility in G20 Electricity Systems*
- **Primary RQ:** Across G20 electricity systems, to what extent is recorded green-bond issuance aligned with distinct annual physical transition outcomes: renewable addition, coal contraction, absolute fossil contraction, and coal contraction without gas- or oil-based offsetting?
- **Population:** 19 country members of the G20; the European Union is not a separate observation.
- **Period:** 2006–2024, with 2005 used only to construct annual physical changes.
- **Evidence level:** Descriptive and associational.
- **Primary endpoint:** Annual absolute total-fossil-electricity contraction.
- **Main exposure:** Recorded green-bond issuance in basis points of nominal GDP.

## Binding scientific boundaries

1. Issuance is assigned to the issuer's country of incorporation. It is not evidence of the destination, sector or additionality of proceeds.
2. No causal financing-effect, project-delivery or domestic-use-of-proceeds claim is permitted.
3. Physical outcomes remain separately visible. No researcher-weighted credibility index is permitted.
4. Absolute TWh quantities accompany directional or percentage measures.
5. Country and year structure, CRV3 cluster-jackknife inference, wild-cluster bootstrap sensitivity, multiplicity and one-country influence must be addressed as locked in the decision log.
6. The research question, primary endpoint, period and main architecture may not change after results without explicit change control.

## Separation from prior manuscripts

The study must not present indicator-boundary validity, breadth/depth concentration, transition matrices, persistence/spells or coal-offset decomposition as a second primary contribution. Within-fossil offsetting is one comparative physical margin inside the finance-alignment question.

## Manuscript rules

- Natural, objective, impersonal Q1-level English.
- Times New Roman throughout the Word manuscript.
- Abstract unnumbered and 140–150 words.
- Introduction 950–1000 words.
- All substantive section and subsection titles numbered, but use one flat Word heading style rather than Heading 1/Heading 2 hierarchy.
- Concise, self-contained table and figure captions; every table and figure cited in the main-text flow.
- Canonical LaTeX for equations during drafting; variables and indices defined at first use.
- No first-person `we`, `our` or `us` unless a target journal requires it.
- No submission-package tailoring until the target journal is selected.

## Change control

If implementation conflicts with a locked decision: stop, document the conflict and evidence, state the scientific consequence, and obtain explicit approval before revising the design. An unattractive or null result is not a reason to change the architecture.
