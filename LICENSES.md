# Licenses and source terms

The MIT License in `LICENSE` applies only to original code in this repository.

- Our World in Data Energy data retain the license and attribution terms documented by the provider and its codebook.
- World Bank indicators retain the World Bank's data terms and required attribution.
- IMF Climate Change Indicators Dashboard material retains IMF and underlying-provider terms. The dataset identifies Refinitiv/LSEG, country authorities, and IMF staff calculations as sources.
- Journal articles, reports, and provider documentation cited in the manuscript are not redistributed by this repository.

No repository license overrides a source provider's rights. Users are responsible for checking the current provider terms before redistributing source snapshots or derived records.
